# Code of Conduct

YAAW Collab contributors are expected to keep technical collaboration professional, specific, and reviewable.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and patches, not people.
- State uncertainty when evidence is incomplete.
- Provide reproduction steps for defects when possible.
- Keep security reports out of public issues when disclosure could increase risk.
- Respect maintainers' decisions on project scope and release risk.
- Give credit for prior work and contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, credential exposure, malicious dependency changes, or knowingly misleading technical claims are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

Security-sensitive concerns should follow `SECURITY.md`.
